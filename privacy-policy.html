Privacy Policy

CareerX (com.careerx.app)

This Privacy Policy explains how CareerX ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application. By downloading, accessing, or using CareerX, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Personal Identification: Name, email address, and phone number when you register an account or sign in.
  • Academic & Career Profile: Educational stream, graduation year, resume data, assessment scores, and bookmarked notes that you voluntarily input.
  • Device & Diagnostics Data: Non-personally identifiable information such as app crash logs, performance diagnostics, operating system version, and anonymous installation IDs.

2. When and How We Collect Information

We collect personal information directly when you:

  • Create an account or sign in using Firebase Authentication or Google Sign-In.
  • Build or edit an ATS resume using our resume tools.
  • Participate in mock tests, quizzes, or career roadmaps.
  • Submit feedback, report an issue, or contact our support team.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely to:

  • Provide, maintain, and optimize application features and user accounts.
  • Generate ATS-friendly resumes and export them in PDF format to your device.
  • Deliver personalized career roadmaps and track mock test progress.
  • Notify you about relevant hiring drives, interview tips, and application updates via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Diagnose crashes and improve app stability and security.

4. Device Permissions

To perform certain core tasks, CareerX may request device permissions:

  • Storage Access (Scoped): Required solely to save your generated Resume PDFs or download study materials to your device. We do not inspect, read, or upload other personal files from your phone.
  • Notifications: Used solely to deliver optional alerts regarding mock exams, job updates, and important announcements. You can disable notifications anytime in your device settings.

5. Third-Party Services

CareerX integrates with trusted, industry-standard third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google LLC): Secure user registration and token-based authentication.
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore (Google LLC): Secure cloud database storage for user profile, test history, and notes.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC): Secure push notification delivery.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crash Reporting (Google LLC): Anonymous diagnostic and performance monitoring.
  • Google Play Services: Core application updates and in-app review prompts.

These third parties handle your data under their own strict privacy policies and confidentiality agreements.

6. Data Protection & Security

We value your trust and implement industry-standard safeguards to protect your personal information. All communications between CareerX and our cloud services are encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS encryption. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

7. Account Deletion and Data Retention

You have the complete right to request the permanent deletion of your CareerX account and all associated personal data at any time.

To request account deletion:

  • Send an email to careerx.app@gmail.com from your registered email address.
  • Subject line: Account Deletion Request - CareerX.
  • Include your registered email address and name.

Upon receipt and verification of your request, your account, profile data, test history, and saved resumes will be permanently deleted from our database within 7 business days.

8. Children's Privacy

CareerX is designed for students, job seekers, and working professionals. Our application is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any updates. Continued use of the application following the posting of changes constitutes acceptance of those changes.
